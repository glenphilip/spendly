Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address and a password (stored in hashed form). As you use Spendly, we store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to provide and improve Spendly: to sign you in, to record and display your expenses, and to generate the summaries and breakdowns you see in the app. We do not sell your personal data or use your expense records for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on servers we control and is protected with reasonable technical and organisational safeguards. We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal data and expense records, except where we are required by law to retain it.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when pages are viewed. Apart from services needed to run the app, we do not share your personal data with third parties, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, please contact us at support@example.com.
